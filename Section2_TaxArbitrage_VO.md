## Structural notes

Four requested sections delivered. Two structural decisions, both for screen-recording pacing:

**Split the transition from the exit setup** (2.0 / 2.1). The bridge out of Section 1 lands on the Scenario B tab; the exit input is on the Comparison tab. One combined beat would have you talking over a tab switch. Split gives the cut a clean seam.

**Added 2.5 as an optional closer.** The "Why this matters" reveal lives on this tab and was built for this video, so leaving it undocumented seemed wrong. Marked optional because the brief calls for a strictly capital-mechanics register and this is a levity beat. Cut it and 2.4 still closes cleanly.

**Changed from the previous draft:** 2.4 now ends by dragging to $100M rather than gesturing vaguely at scale — the two anchor numbers from the brief now appear on screen exactly, so it's worth showing them. 2.1 gained an explicit line that the exit is not an ARR multiple, since that is the substantive change to the model.

## What changed in the UI

1. **Exit is now a dollar input.** The slider was an ARR multiple (2×–9× of $13.2M); it is now a direct dollar amount, $10M–$200M in $5M steps. No implied revenue, no scaling against ARR. Footnote reads "Set independently of ARR." Applies to both the Scenario B and Comparison tabs, which share the input.
2. **`DOMAIN_DEAL_SHARE` = 23.04%**, was 25%.
3. **Arbitrage stated directly** as `domainValue × 17%`, with the spread derived as `ORDINARY_RATE − DOMAIN_GAINS_RATE` so it can't drift from the rates above it. Arithmetically identical to the previous `taxA − taxB`, now explicit.
4. **Exact figures on screen.** The assumption note shows `23.04%` and the dollar value; the advantage card shows `+$3,916,800` rather than a rounded `$3.9M`. Both numbers are voiced, so both are legible on camera.

Defaults: exit **$50M**, Comparison tab on load.

**Untouched:** the Scenario B tab still derives DomainCo's value from the licensing model (annual royalty × IP multiple), which at 8% of Operating Profit and 8× gives $5.1M — about 10% of a $50M deal. That path is independent of the Comparison's flat 23.04% and the directive did not ask me to reconcile them. If you want the Scenario B tab to use 23.04% as well, say so and I'll wire it through.
