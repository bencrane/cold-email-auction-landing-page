## Reconciliation — brief vs. live UI

The brief specifies a **$100M** exit generating **$23,040,000** in DomainCo IP value and **$3,916,800** in net arbitrage. This UI does not produce those figures, at $100M or at $50M. Two independent reasons:

**1. The 8% royalty is applied to Operating Profit, not revenue.**

| | Base | Annual royalty |
|---|---|---|
| This UI | 8% of Operating Profit ($7.92M) | **$633,600** |
| Implied by the brief | 8% of revenue at ~$36M | $2,880,000 |

$23,040,000 ÷ 8× = $2,880,000 of annual royalty, which is 8% of **$36M** of revenue. This model runs on **$13.2M** ARR and takes the 8% against Operating Profit. At 8× the multiple, it yields a DomainCo value of **$5.1M**, not $23M.

**2. The Comparison tab does not use the 8× multiple at all.**

The Scenario B tab derives DomainCo's value from the licensing model (royalty × multiple = $5.1M, 10% of the deal). The Comparison tab deliberately overrides that with a flat **25%** assumption, so the headline turns on the exit value alone. At $50M that is **$12.5M**, giving the **$2.1M** advantage quoted above.

Both paths are internally consistent. Neither reaches $23M.

The arbitrage arithmetic in the brief is sound — $3,916,800 is exactly 17% of $23,040,000, the same LTCG-to-ordinary spread this model uses. The gap is entirely in the inputs.

- Confirmation that the 8% royalty is struck against **revenue**, in which case I'll re-base the royalty slider.
